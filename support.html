Common questions

  • Is my data backed up? Yes — your workouts sync automatically to your personal iCloud. Reinstalling or switching phones restores everything. You can also export a backup file in Settings → Data.
  • How do I import from Hevy or Strong? Export a CSV from that app, then use Settings → Data → Import. Step-by-step instructions appear in the app before you pick the file.
  • Why isn't a muscle lighting up? The avatar lights the muscles your logged exercises actually train, scaled to your recent training. Tap any muscle to see its set count for the week.
  • Heart rate during workouts? Live heart rate needs compatible AirPods and the Apple Health connection turned on (Settings → Apple Health).
  • Managing your subscription: Subscriptions are billed through Apple. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Restore a purchase: On the Premium screen, tap "Restore Purchases."

Found a bug?

Email a description (and a screenshot if you can) to stefflercj@gmail.com. Crash reports reach us automatically and anonymously, but a note about what you were doing helps us fix things faster.

Privacy
