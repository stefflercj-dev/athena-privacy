The log

Fast enough to use between sets.

Last week's numbers sit greyed out in the row you're filling, so you always know what to beat. The rest timer starts itself. A PR badge lands the second you break one. Supersets group and rest as a pair.

Four hundred exercises in the library, or build your own.

Your circle

Your friends see your body as it is.

Not a feed of gym selfies. A row of bodies, each one lit by what that person actually trained this week, and dark everywhere they didn't. You can tell who skipped legs without anyone saying a word.

Add people with a six-character code or a QR, then open their card: level, title, banner, streak.
