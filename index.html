ATHENA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

ATHENA is a strength-training tracker. This policy explains, in plain terms, what data the app handles.

The short version: Your workout data is yours. It lives on your device and in your personal iCloud — we can’t see it. No ads, no third-party analytics or trackers, and we never sell your data.

What the app stores

  • Your training data — workouts, exercises, sets, weights, routines, body-weight entries, and settings. Stored on your device and synced through your own iCloud account (Apple’s CloudKit) so you don’t lose it when you change phones. We, the developer, have no access to this — it lives in your private iCloud.
  • Profile basics — an optional name and goal you enter, stored the same way.

Crews (optional social feature)

If you create or join a Crew (an invite-only group), the app shares a limited summary with that crew’s members:

  • A display name you choose
  • Weekly training summaries — which muscle groups you trained, your streak, and set counts (never your specific weights, exercises, or notes)

These are stored in a shared iCloud database so your crew members can see them. You can turn sharing off or leave a crew at any time, which removes your shared data. If you report a member, we receive that report (the accounts involved and the reason) so we can review and act on it.

What we do NOT do

  • No ads or ad networks
  • No third-party analytics or tracking
  • No selling or sharing your data with anyone
  • No account or login — your identity is your anonymous iCloud ID

Notifications

The app can send local reminders (training-day nudges, rest-timer alerts). These are generated on your device; we don’t see them. You control them in iOS Settings.

Your control & deletion

  • Delete any data in the app at any time
  • Delete the app to remove its local data; the iCloud copy is managed in Settings → [your name] → iCloud
  • Turn off Crew sharing or leave a crew to remove your shared summaries

Children

ATHENA is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “last updated” date above will change.
